New settings (merged):
- T14 by stratified sample against a Bergum / ASTM E2709 limit (F0229, bromocriptine). Content uniformity is tested on 60 tablets (3 tablets taken at each of 20 times in the run). The batch passes when the RSD is within a limit that depends on the observed mean, the sample size and the stated confidence and coverage. The source's limits were 4.52, 4.01 and 4.85 % RSD for means of 101.5, 103.0 and 100.5 %. So the limit is computed per batch, not typed as a constant. Variant of T14 (it has a large-N option but no acceptance limit that moves with the mean).

No GAP.

Safety rules:
- One granulation, several strengths (F0225, niacin): the same base granulation makes four strengths, and the share of granulation in the tablet differs by strength (74.0, 81.0, 89.0 and 95.5 %). If the 1000 mg blend takes another strength's extragranular ratio and is compressed at the 1000 mg weight (1203.6 mg), each tablet holds about 932 mg niacin with the 750 mg ratio and about 849 mg with the 500 mg ratio (-7 % and -15 %). Before compression the master must check niacin per tablet against the label: tablet weight x granulation share x granulation assay (C1 around a computed value).
- Water activity as a dose-critical limit (F0234, levothyroxine/liothyronine, narrow therapeutic index). The source's tablets at water activity 0.45 kept 88.6 % of the label after 12 months, against 99.6 % at 0.3 in sealed packs. The end-of-drying and before-packing water activity limits fit C1, but they decide the dose at expiry. Like the wave-1 realgar limit, they must not be widened without QA approval.
- Reminders of known rules:
  - A typed salt quantity that does not match the molecular weights (F0229, known wave 1/2): 0.945 mg bromocriptine mesylate holds 0.824 mg base, 3 % above the 0.8 mg claim. It is refused unless it is declared as a T16 overage.
  - A stated total below the sum of the lines (F0236, known wave 1/2): compressing to the stated 200 mg when the lines add up to 230 mg gives 65 mg levosulpiride in place of 75 mg (-13 %).
  - Label basis per strength and market (known wave 1): lercanidipine HCl against base (about 6 %, F0221), prazosin HCl against base (about 9.5 %, F0226), metoclopramide as base or as HCl monohydrate (about 18 %, F0237), and magnesium omeprazole with 2 drug molecules per salt unit (factor 1.032; read as one molecule per salt unit it would be about twice that, F0238).
  - Dry polymer read as dispersion (F0238, T18 % solids): the 9.00 kg methacrylic acid copolymer is dry polymer, since triethyl citrate at 30 % of it only fits that reading. Weighing 9.00 kg of a 30 % dispersion would apply 30 % of the enteric layer, so acid resistance fails and omeprazole is lost in the stomach.
  - Double adjustment (F0222, F0238, known wave 1): the compression weight comes from the granule or pellet assay after T9 at dispensing, so it is limited to a stated band.
  - Several activities in one material (F0240, known wave 2): when pancreatin is dosed by lipase units, protease and amylase follow and must be shown and checked.
