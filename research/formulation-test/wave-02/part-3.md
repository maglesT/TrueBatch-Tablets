Safety rules:
- Dosing a solution by fill weight (F0157, selegiline lyophilisate): each unit's dose is fill weight × mix concentration. Water gained or lost while mixing under vacuum and heat changes every unit's dose. The bulk mass, made up with T1 "Difference", or a bulk assay must pass before dosing starts (F5 hold, F3 if out). Read literally, the source's water (15.73 kg + 1.5 L) against its 87.4 % formula gives about 7.7 % less drug per unit unless the extra water is lost.
- Dose set by the print design (F0154, 3D printing): the same 12 × 8 mm tablet held 235 mg at 25 % infill and 335 mg at 50 % infill (about 43 % more). The master must check the design and slicer file identity (C4) as well as the printed weight. The target weight comes from the filament's measured content (T18 in-process). The known wave-1 double-adjustment rule applies: no second potency adjustment at dispensing.
- Stated total below the sum of the lines (F0159, dacomitinib): compressing to the stated 150 mg instead of the 200 mg the lines add up to gives 75 mg instead of 100 mg (−25 %). This is the known wave-1 rule (theoretical weight is the T2 sum of the weighed lines), recorded as a reminder.
- Base vs hydrate not named (F0159): the example does not say free base or monohydrate (factor 1.038). Covered by C5 when the master states the basis; a reminder.

Sources that contradict themselves:
- EP 0 814 789 B2 (F0157): the batch uses 15.73 kg of water plus another 1.5 L, but the unit formula (87.4 % water in an 18 kg batch) allows 15.73 kg in total. The text is also unclear about which vessel the drug is added to.
- Lamichhane et al., Pharmaceutics 2019 (F0154): the methods give a 0.4 mm shell for open and closed systems, but Table 3 gives 0.6 mm for the open systems F1-F3. The extruder torque is printed as "50-60 nm" (N·m meant).
- EP 1 746 999 B1 (F0159): the subtotal of 0.148 g and the tablet total of 0.150 g do not match the lines, which add up to 0.198 g + 0.002 g Mg stearate = 0.200 g. The "1 %" Mg stearate fits 200 mg, and the per-10,000 column has the same error (1,480 / 1,500 g against 1,980 / 2,000 g).
- WO 2018/048847 / AU 2017324844 B2 (F0160): Table 9 is headed "150 mg compression runs", but its columns are labelled "25 mg 0109" and "25 mg 0110" at about 600 mg tablet weight (the 150 mg weight). The coating table uses the same "25 mg" labels.
- EP 0 966 287 B1 (F0153): the formula line says "polyvinylpyrrolidone", while the process text calls it PVP PXL, a disintegrant (crospovidone). MCC is added both before and after granulation, but only one quantity is given.
- EP 1 077 691 B1 (F0152): 312 mg granules + 7 % L-valine = 333.8 mg, but the tablet is stated as 336 mg (0.6 %, minor; it may be residual water).
