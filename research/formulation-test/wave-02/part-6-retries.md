## F0125 verapamil hydrochloride, press-coated pulsatile (chronotherapy) tablet, melt granulation, 180 mg

Source: US 9,283,192 B2, Methods section, Tables 1 and 2, https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/5f/88/71/4775c5ad7d27b5/US9283192.pdf. Close variant: press-coated tablet with a melt-granulated core and coat (listed film-coated, direct compression); lab scale (6 tablets).

- Core: verapamil HCl 194.5 mg (= 180 mg verapamil) with glycerol behenate and lactose; batch 5.835 g + 0.7 g + 0.7 g; core stated as 234.5 mg, 10 mm die at 2 t for 1 min.
- Melt granulation: glycerol behenate melted at 100 C, lactose and drug stirred in to a sticky mass, stirred until cool, left at least 30 min, forced through 1 mm and collected on 500 um (only the 500 um-1 mm fraction used).
- Coat granules: glycerol behenate 6 g and L-HPC LH-32 14 g (30:70) made the same way; 250 mg below and 250 mg above the centred core in a 13 mm die at 5 t for 2 min (734.5 mg tablet).
- Dissolution (pH 7 phosphate, 900 ml): lag about 3.5 h, over 90 % released by about 7 h; LH-21 instead of LH-32 shortens the lag to about 1 h.
- Batch 200,000 tablets on a press-coater. Standard practice assumed: heated high-shear melt granulation and cooling, core-centring checks, an HPMC film coat at 3 % for the listed film-coated type, IPC limits.

Safety rules:
- Granules cut to a size fraction (F0125). Only the 500 um-1 mm part of the melt granulate is used. If the drug is not spread evenly across sizes, the kept fraction no longer has the formula's drug content. The core weight must come from the kept fraction's assay (T18 in-process), with the known wave-1 limit when the API was already potency-adjusted, and the rejected fractions must appear in the T4 reconciliation. No new template.
- Stated core weight against the batch formula (F0125). Table 1 gives 241.2 mg cores for 194.5 mg verapamil HCl. Compressing to the stated 234.5 mg gives 189.1 mg HCl (175.1 mg base, -2.7 %). Known wave-1 rule (theoretical weight = T2 sum of the lines); a reminder.
- % of which stage (F0184). Table 1 is % of the coated tablet. Applying 26.5 % to the 360 mg core gives 95.4 mg instead of 100 mg (-4.6 %). Known wave-2 rule (weight targets must name their stage), here for a % base; a reminder.
- Salt and hydrate basis (F0195). The 30.71 mg of L-methylfolate calcium is 24.0 mg free acid if it is the pentahydrate (-4.0 %) and 28.4 mg if anhydrous (+13.4 %); the example does not say which. The patent's commercial 15 mg tablets target 19.5 mg (30 % overfill), which must be a declared T16 overage. Known rules (T11 typed-factor check, wave 1; declared overage, wave 2); reminders.
- Label basis (F0193, F0165). 16.66 % of 300 mg is 49.98 mg sildenafil citrate (35.6 mg base): in a product labelled 50 mg as base it is 29 % low. Cyclobenzaprine 5.0 mg HCl is 4.42 mg base (-12 %) if the claim is "as free base", as the description says. Known label-basis rule; reminders.
- Element basis (F0197). 420 mg MgO is 253 mg Mg; the listed "300 mg magnesium citrate" is a salt figure. Covered by C5 "as element"; a reminder.

Sources that contradict themselves:
- US 9,283,192 B2 (F0125): Table 1 (5.835 g drug for 30 cores of 194.5 mg, plus 0.7 g + 0.7 g) gives 241.2 mg cores, but the core is stated as 234.5 mg; the UV wavelength is printed as "279 mm".
- El-Masry & Helmy 2020 (F0140): every Table 1 formulation adds up to 1,000 mg, but tablet weights are reported as 0.196-0.218 g; hardness is given in g/cm3, with different ranges in the text (4.59-5.88) and the table (4.14-6.17); the press is called a "12-station single punch" machine.
- WO 99/18937 A1 (F0165): the description says the 5 mg is taken as the free base, while the example and claim 12 give 5.0 mg of the hydrochloride; every line is given as an average within +/- 20 %.
- RU 2 403 045 C2 (F0176): the method text puts 4.9 % starch and 0.72 % silica in the granules and 3.3 % and 1.08 % outside, but Table 1 has the opposite split (69.9 g in / 98.1 g out; 21.0 g in / 14.0 g out). The 32.6 L of water for the methylcellulose cannot belong to the 2.03 kg batches of Table 1 and no other batch size is given.
- RU 2 756 312 C1 (F0184): Table 1 % (roxadustat 26.5) on "360 mg tablets with 100 mg" works only if the % refer to the coated tablet and 360 mg to the core; read on the 360 mg it is 95.4 mg.
- Drug Des Devel Ther 2025, avanafil (F0191): factor X3 is called the % of starch in the diluent mixture, but Table 3 starch is that % of mannitol alone; a drug content of 87.9 % (F5) is described as meeting USP criteria.
- Pharmaceutics 2021;13:870 (F0193): the process never says when the drug is added (only mannitol and MCC are blended and granulated); citric acid is blended in but is not in any composition table; the optimisation table gives friability up to 1.2 % while Table 8 has 1.30 %.
- US 2015/0030676 A1 (F0195): 16.6 % of 185 mg (30.71 mg) matches neither form of the calcium salt for "25 mg relative to free acid"; the tooling for a 185 mg tablet is given as "0.63 mm".
